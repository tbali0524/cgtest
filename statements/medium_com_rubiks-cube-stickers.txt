Goal
Your Rubik's Cube is scrambled and some of the stickers have come off!

You still don't know how to solve a Rubik's Cube, but you'd like to stick the stickers back on correctly so that when you finally learn how, it will still be possible...

The order of the colors of the solved cube is given in the first row.
For example, W G R B O Y means :

WWW
WWW
WWW
---
GGG|RRR|BBB|OOO
GGG|RRR|BBB|OOO
GGG|RRR|BBB|OOO
---
YYY
YYY
YYY

Note: In a Rubik's Cube, the centers are linked; their order cannot be changed...

Input
Line 1 : 6 characters U F R B L D, separated by spaces, representing the colors of the Cube's faces in order: Up, Front, Right, Back, Left, Down.
11 following lines : the colors of the stickers for each of the 27 small squares of the Rubik's Cube with a "." for the stickers that have been removed.

Output
11 lines : the colors of the stickers for each of the 27 small squares of the Rubik's Cube. (The Cube must not have rotated.)

Constraints
All Rubik's Cubes are guaranteed to be solvable, but you aren't required to solve them.
U, F, R, B, L and D are all distinct.

Example

Input
W G R B O Y
ORO
ROR
ORO
---
WYW|BGB|YWY|GBG
YWY|GBG|WYW|B.B
WYW|BGB|YWY|GBG
---
ROR
ORO
ROR

Output
ORO
ROR
ORO
---
WYW|BGB|YWY|GBG
YWY|GBG|WYW|BGB
WYW|BGB|YWY|GBG
---
ROR
ORO
ROR
